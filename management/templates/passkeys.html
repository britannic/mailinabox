Passkeys

Passkeys let you sign in to this control panel without a password, using your device's fingerprint, face, screen lock, or a security key. A passkey is phishing-resistant and counts as two-factor authentication on its own, so you are not asked for a six-digit code when you sign in with one.

Your password still works everywhere, so adding or removing passkeys can never lock you out of your account.
